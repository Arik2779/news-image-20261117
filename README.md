# news-image-20261117 — Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image".

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper front page for Tuesday
17 November 2026: masthead "WORLD NEWS" with the date, and four illustrated
panels, each verified against reporting from 17 November 2026 (see
`../RESEARCH.md`):

1. **KERCH BRIDGE HIT — ROAD SPAN DOWN.** Ukrainian drones and missiles
   struck the Kerch Bridge overnight 16–17 November; the road arch and a
   road span collapsed into the Kerch Strait, the road link out of action.
   Second strike on the bridge in two nights.
2. **TANKERS ON FIRE IN HORMUZ — IRAN BLAMES MINES.** A fourth tanker
   caught fire on Tuesday 17 November in/near the Strait of Hormuz.
   Iran's navy says the tankers hit mines on unauthorised routes; the mine
   cause is Iran's claim and could not be independently verified, so the
   headline attributes it ("Iran blames mines") rather than stating it as
   settled fact.
3. **SUMMIT SET: TRUMP, XI, PUTIN MEET THURSDAY.** China's foreign
   ministry confirmed Xi will host Trump and Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday
   19 November — the first trilateral meeting of the three leaders; on
   17 November the agenda was reported largely finalised and Trump was in
   Manila on his Asia trip.
4. **VULCAN FAILS: 40 SATELLITES LOST.** ULA's Vulcan rocket failed on
   Sunday 15 November, losing 40 Amazon Leo satellites; on Tuesday
   17 November ULA gave its first detailed account — early data pointed to
   low first-stage booster performance just after liftoff.

The image is a JPEG in a file named `entry.png`, which the rules allow
(the format is told apart by its first bytes). 1024×1024 px, 314,306 bytes
(under the 1 MiB limit).
